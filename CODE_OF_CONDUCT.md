# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 3.0.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all. We are committed to respecting the dignity,
rights, and contributions of every participant.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors are expected to engage kindly and honestly, respect different viewpoints, give and accept constructive
feedback gracefully, take responsibility for their actions, and help repair harm when it occurs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, demeaning attacks, inappropriate sexualized behaviour, sharing private information,
threats, impersonation, and conduct that undermines community safety are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately through https://www.hauntedmc.nl/support, contact@remyduijsens.com, or GitHub's reporting
tools. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially where possible and may respond with a warning, limited access,
temporary suspension, or permanent ban depending on severity and pattern.

## Scope and attribution

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).
